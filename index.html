Experience

  • Kaluza

    Head of Design

    Apr 2021 – Present

    I’m Head of Design within Kaluza’s global, multidisciplinary UX team across the UK, Portugal and Australia, shaping the experience vision for a complex, multi-product energy platform serving global retailers including AGL, Shell Energy Australia and ENGIE. I defined our UX AI strategy, built a shared quality framework grounded in research and analytics, and relaunched our design system as a global-ready component and pattern library. I established the Australian design function in Melbourne and keep a distributed global team aligned through coaching and a culture of trust and collaboration.

    Lead Designer

    Dec 2019 – Apr 2021

    During Kaluza’s start-up phase, I led UX on the migration of 3.5 million customers from SSE Energy Services onto the platform, one of the most ambitious digital migrations in UK energy retail. I designed end-to-end billing, payments and account journeys, driving a 50% improvement in agent efficiency and a 32% reduction in handling time. I also grew the team from five to more than 25 as the business scaled, achieving an 8.1 out of 10 engagement score.

  • OVO Energy

    Senior Designer

    Dec 2017 – Dec 2019

    At OVO Energy I designed a new customer-facing account experience from first principles, migrating 100,000 smart meter accounts by the end of 2018 and over 90% of customers onto the new platform by H1 2020. We delivered a fully compliant plan renewal journey in six weeks, and explored real-time billing concepts from smart meter data that laid foundations for time-of-use tariffs and propositions such as solar and EVs. I created OVO's flexible PDF bill template, still in production today and used to generate hundreds of millions of bills. I also raised key digital journeys to a Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT) of 4 out of 5. I mapped OVO's first service blueprint and authored its first accessibility principles, informed by in-home and lab research. This work became the foundations of the Kaluza platform.

  • Environment Agency

    Interaction Designer

    Oct 2016 – Nov 2017

    Interaction Designer on the GOV.UK waste permitting service, where an agile, cross-functional team replaced a fragmented set of PDF forms with a single online application built to Government Digital Service standards. Iterated through continuous user research, the service passed its GDS assessment against the Service Standard and went on to handle 95% of applications. I also completed GDS training and contributed to cross-government design community events.

  • IOP (Institute of Physics) Publishing

    UX Designer

    Jan 2010 – Oct 2016

    As IOP Publishing's first UX Designer, I established a design system and brought a coherent experience across a digital portfolio of scientific journals, eBooks and Physics World. My work won a 2015 Eddie Digital Award (Best App and Digital Edition, Physics World) and a 2016 Drum Online Media Award for the American Astronomical Society news site.

  • Self-employed
